Student activities

Three short activities. Each one uses the same simulated material models as the laboratory, so your answers can be checked against the simulation.

All values are simulated for teaching. The material models are representative of the named grades and are not design data. Standards are referenced conceptually (ISO 6892-1, ASTM E8/E8M); this is not a standards-compliant test.
